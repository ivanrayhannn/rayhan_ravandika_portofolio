Case study · PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk. · 2026 to present

Micro credit, from application to restructuring

As a Micro Credit Analyst I follow a loan through its whole life: checking that the business is feasible, that it can repay, that the documents and collateral hold up, and what to do when payments slow down. These are the five processes I work with.

A general view of the micro credit process. Amounts are examples, not customer data, and nothing here shows Bank Mandiri's internal policy.

01

01. Business feasibility and credit assessment

Before any number is calculated, I need to know whether the business is real, running and worth lending to.

  1. 1Step 1: Identify the business profile
  2. 2Step 2: Analyse how the business operates
  3. 3Step 3: Assess financial performance
  4. 4Step 4: Check credit historyin SLIK, the OJK credit bureau
  5. 5Step 5: Evaluate repayment capacity
  6. 6Step 6: Credit recommendation

Recommendation

Approve credit only if the business is feasible and its repayment capacity is adequate.

Key learning

A proper feasibility check is the first and cheapest way to keep credit risk low.

02

02. Financial analysis and repayment capacity

The question is simple: after running the business, is there enough cash left to pay the installment? Example figures per month:

  1. Business revenueSales the business brings inRp45M
  2. Operational costStock, wages, rent and other running costs− Rp29M
  3. Net profitWhat the business keepsRp16M
  4. Compare with credit obligationsExisting installments plus the proposed one
  5. Repayment capacity conclusionThe installment must fit inside the cash flow

Recommendation

Make sure the installment stays within the business cash flow.

Key learning

High revenue does not always mean strong repayment ability. Profit after costs is what pays the loan.

03

03. Collateral and legal document verification

A good business still needs valid documents and collateral that legally belongs to the borrower.

  1. 1Step 1: Collect documents
  2. 2Step 2: Verify identity
  3. 3Step 3: Check collateral ownership
  4. 4Step 4: Ensure legal compliance
  5. 5Step 5: Complete the binding processAPHT, the collateral deed
  6. 6Step 6: Final review

Recommendation

Proceed only when every document is valid and complete.

Key learning

Accuracy in documentation is critical for risk mitigation.

04

04. Early warning and credit collection strategy

When a payment is late, the first job is to understand why before deciding what to do next.

  1. 1Step 1: Identify delinquency
  2. 2Step 2: Communicate with the debtor
  3. 3Step 3: Analyse the root cause
  4. 4Step 4: Assess payment ability
  5. YesMonitor paymentNoEscalate and find a solution

Recommendation

Take follow-up action that fits the debtor's condition.

Key learning

Every overdue account has a different reason. Spotting it early is key.

05

05. Credit restructuring analysis

If the business has slowed down but can recover, restructuring can keep the loan alive on terms the borrower can meet.

  1. 1Step 1: Identify the financial decline
  2. 2Step 2: Analyse current ability
  3. 3Step 3: Explore restructuring options
  4. 4Step 4: Simulate the new scheme
  5. 5Step 5: Provide a recommendation

Recommendation

Restructure with a realistic and sustainable installment plan.

Key learning

Restructuring aims for long-term repayment, not just a lower installment.
