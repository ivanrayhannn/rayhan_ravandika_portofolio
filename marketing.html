Business rules

The rules that keep the game fair for customers and inside budget for marketing. Each one is built into the prototype above.

  1. Weights must total 100%

    If they don't, the game pauses and customers see a short message instead of a broken wheel.

  2. Stock caps the budget

    A prize with no stock left can't be won. Its share of the odds goes to "Try again".

  3. Spins per customer

    Each customer gets a set number of spins per day. When they run out, the button says so and tells them when to come back.

  4. One code per win

    Every voucher gets its own code, valid for 7 days, so finance can trace each redemption to one spin.

Campaign flow

  1. Set upMarketing picks prizes, weights, stock and dates
  2. SpinCustomer spins within their daily limit
  3. IssueA unique voucher code is created
  4. RedeemCode is used at checkout before it expires
  5. ReportSpins, wins and redemptions per prize

What to measure

No results are shown because this page is a prototype. These are the numbers a campaign report would track.

  • Participation

    Customers who spun at least once, out of those who saw the game.

  • Redemption rate

    Vouchers used at checkout, out of vouchers issued, per prize.

  • Cost per redemption

    Prize value spent for each voucher actually used.

  • Repeat visits

    Customers who came back on another day to spin again.
