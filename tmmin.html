Logistics at Toyota Indonesia: milkrun and crossdock

As a Fujitsu consultant dedicated to Toyota Indonesia's logistics, I work on the systems behind how parts move from suppliers to the plant. This page explains two of those flows, milkrun and crossdock, in general terms, and where a business system analyst fits in.

Milkrun: one truck, many suppliers

Instead of every supplier sending its own truck, one truck follows a fixed route and collects parts from several suppliers in one loop, on a fixed schedule. Trucks run fuller, and the plant knows when parts will arrive.

  1. PlanRoutes and a time window for each supplier.
  2. OrderThe parts to collect from each supplier on each cycle.
  3. Pick upThe truck visits the suppliers in a set order.
  4. ArriveAt the crossdock or the plant, inside its time window.
  5. ReturnEmpty packaging goes back to the suppliers on the next loop.

Crossdock: sort, don't store

Parts from many inbound trucks are unloaded, sorted by destination and loaded onto outbound trucks, with little or no storage in between. Milkrun trucks often end their loop here.

  1. ReceiveUnload the inbound truck at its dock.
  2. CheckCompare what arrived with what was ordered.
  3. SortGroup the parts by destination or route.
  4. StageMove each group to its outbound lane.
  5. DispatchLoad the outbound truck and send it on time.

Where systems help

Both flows run on timing and accuracy. This is the kind of support a logistics system gives each step, and where requirements work starts.

StepWhat the business needsWhat a system provides
Route planningFixed routes and time windows per supplierMaster data for routes, suppliers and time windows
PickupKnow exactly what to collect on each cycleA pickup list per supplier per cycle
ReceivingConfirm what arrived against what was orderedChecks against the order, with flags for shortages and extras
SortingSend the right parts to the right outbound truckA destination for every part and route
MonitoringSee late trucks and shortages earlyDashboards of arrivals against time windows
