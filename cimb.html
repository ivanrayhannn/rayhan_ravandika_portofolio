Expense management: reimbursement, cash advance, petty cash and accruals

CIMB Niaga ran its employee spending on the web-based Jojonomic expense platform. As Business Analyst I worked on its modules and on the hand-offs between them: how money moves from one module to the next.

What I did

CIMB Niaga needed several modules improved to match how its teams actually spend and settle money.

The four modules

Each module covers a different way an employee spends company money. Finance needs all four to end up in one consistent set of numbers.

Where the modules meet

A large part of the work sat in these hand-offs. If one of them drops a record or posts it twice, finance sees a wrong balance at month end.

A cash advance from request to close

  1. RequestEmployee asks for an advance
  2. ApproveApproval chain by cost centre
  3. DisburseFinance pays the advance out
  4. SpendEmployee keeps receipts
  5. SettleClaim with receipts is matched to the advance
  6. CloseDifference returned or paid out

Where two modules meet: settling a cash advance

The settlement screen puts the advance and the actual spending side by side, so the employee and finance see the same difference before anyone approves it.

  • Spent less than the advance: the employee returns the difference.
  • Spent more than the advance: the extra is paid back as a reimbursement.
  • Receipts missing: the settlement can't be sent until each line has one.

Example acceptance criteria, cash advance settlement

As an employee, I want to settle my advance with receipts so the difference is returned or paid without a separate claim.

  1. Given an advance of Rp2,000,000, when I settle Rp1,750,000 of receipts, then the screen shows "You return Rp250,000" and the advance stays open until the transfer is confirmed.
  2. Given the same advance, when I settle Rp2,300,000, then Rp300,000 is created as a reimbursement and the advance closes.
  3. Given a settlement line without a receipt, when I tap Send, then that line shows "Add a receipt for this item" and nothing is sent.
