Case study · FWD Insurance, via Jojonomic · 2022 to 2023

Fitting a standard HRIS to company policy with custom modules

FWD Insurance used the Jojonomic HRIS, but several of its HR rules didn't fit the standard modules. As Business Analyst I worked out where the product could be configured and where it needed a custom module, with overtime under FWD's own policy as a main example.

The policy rules, screens and numbers on this page are examples recreated for the portfolio. They show the kind of rule a custom module handles, not FWD Insurance's actual policy.

What I did

The question on every request was the same: can the standard module do this with settings, or does FWD need its own logic?

  1. Requirements

    Gathered FWD's HR rules from the business users and prioritised them for development.

  2. Gap analysis

    Compared each rule with the standard HRIS module and decided between configuration and a custom module.

  3. Meetings and reviews

    Facilitated stakeholder meetings, sprint planning, requirement reviews and clarification sessions.

  4. Delivery and testing

    Monitored daily progress and helped resolve data and integration issues during unit testing.

Standard module or custom module?

A gap analysis like this, one row per rule, decided the build. Configuration is cheaper and survives product updates, so a rule only became custom when settings couldn't express it.

Example: overtime with a company policy

HR keeps the policy on the web as plain rules. The mobile app checks every request against them before it is sent, so employees see the outcome up front instead of after a rejection.

The request checks itself against the policy

Before sending, the app compares the planned hours with the employee's grade and caps, then tells them who will approve it.

  • Within the daily cap: goes to the direct manager.
  • Over the daily cap: also goes to the department head, and the screen says why.
  • Over the monthly cap or not eligible: blocked, with the reason and what to do instead.

From policy document to working module

  1. GatherHR rules from FWD users
  2. GapConfigure or custom, rule by rule
  3. SpecRules written as a table developers and QA share
  4. BuildSprints with reviews and daily follow-up
  5. TestUnit tests, data and integration fixes

Example acceptance criteria, daily cap

As HR, I want overtime above the daily cap to need the department head's approval, so long shifts are a deliberate decision.

  1. Given a daily cap of 3 hours, when an employee requests 2 hours, then the request goes to the direct manager only.
  2. Given the same cap, when an employee requests 5 hours, then the screen says "Over the 3-hour daily cap" and the request goes to the manager, then the department head.
  3. Given an employee at 40 of 40 monthly hours, when they open Overtime, then the form shows "Monthly overtime limit reached" and can't be sent.
